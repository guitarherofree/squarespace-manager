First build. It connects to your site and reads it; editing arrives in later updates.

- **Add your site** by its address. The app checks that it's on Squarespace and finds its version (7.0 or 7.1) and template.
- **Live preview** of the site at desktop, tablet and mobile widths.
- **Snapshots** save every page's content and markup and the site's CSS on your Mac, then show what changed since the previous snapshot, including edits made directly in Squarespace.
- **Plan setting**, which decides how publishing will work once editing arrives.
- **Automatic updates.** The app checks for new versions daily. Use Check for Updates… in the app menu at any time.
